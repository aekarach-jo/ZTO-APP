This Privacy Policy explains what personal data the CLS Global mobile application collects, why we collect it, how we use and share it, and the choices and rights you have. By creating an account or using the app you agree to this Policy.

1. Data we collect

Data you provide

  • Account data — name, phone number, email address, password (stored only in hashed form on our servers) and one-time verification codes, used to create and secure your account, sign you in and reset your password.
  • Profile data — profile photo, display name and contact details, used to personalise your account and identify you to our operations staff.
  • Shipment data — sender and recipient names, phone numbers, pickup and delivery addresses, parcel description, weight, dimensions, declared value, tracking numbers and delivery status, used to accept, transport, track and deliver your parcels.
  • Payment data — order amount, currency, payment method, bank reference number, payment status and payment QR codes, used to bill you and confirm payment.
  • Support and chat data — messages and images you send to our support or branch staff through the in-app chat, used to answer your enquiries.
  • Images — photos you take with the camera or select from your gallery, used only for the purpose you chose them for.

We do not collect or store full card numbers, CVV codes or bank login credentials. Payments are completed in your own banking application using a payment QR code; we receive only the transaction reference and status.

Data collected automatically

  • Device and push identifiers — Firebase Cloud Messaging token, device model, operating system version and app version, used to deliver notifications and diagnose errors.
  • Usage and diagnostic data — error logs, request timestamps and feature usage, used to keep the app reliable and secure.
  • Location data — when you tap the button to pin a delivery address, or open the screen that sets your default forwarding address, the app reads your device's current position once so the map opens centred on where you are. The position is used only to place the map pin; it is not tracked in the background, not stored on our servers, and not used for any other purpose. If you have already saved a default address, the map uses that address and your position is not read at all. The address you finally confirm is saved as shipment data.

The app does not collect location in the background or while it is closed.

We do not collect data from children, we do not sell personal data, and we do not use your data for third-party advertising or profiling.

2. Device permissions

You may decline any permission; only the related feature stops working.

  • Camera — to take a profile photo or photograph a document or parcel to send in chat.
  • Photos and storage — to select an existing image, and to save a payment QR code or receipt to your gallery when you tap Save.
  • Notifications — to send you delivery, payment and chat notifications.
  • Location (while using the app) — to centre the map on your current position when you pin a delivery address. If you decline, you can still pin the address manually.

3. How we use your data

  • Provide the service: register your account, create shipping orders, track parcels, process payments and deliver notifications.
  • Communicate with you about your orders, deliveries, payments and support requests.
  • Operate and improve the app: fix bugs, monitor performance and improve features.
  • Protect the service: detect and prevent fraud, abuse and unauthorised access.
  • Comply with legal obligations, including tax, customs, transport and record-keeping requirements in the Lao PDR and any destination country.

Where the GDPR or an equivalent law applies, we rely on performance of a contract (account, shipment and payment data), legitimate interests (security and service improvement), consent (camera, photo and notification access, withdrawable at any time in your device settings) and legal obligation (retention of transport and financial records).

4. How we share your data

  • Delivery and logistics partners, including partner carriers and customs agents, receive recipient name, address, phone number and parcel details in order to complete cross-border shipments.
  • Payment and banking providers, to generate payment QR codes and confirm transactions.
  • Service providers acting on our behalf under a data-processing agreement: Google (Firebase Cloud Messaging) for push delivery, Google Maps Platform for map display, and our hosting provider.
  • Authorities, where disclosure is required by law, court order, or customs and transport regulation.
  • A successor entity, if our business is merged, acquired or reorganised.

We never sell your personal data and we never share it for third-party advertising.

5. International transfers

Parcels cross borders, so shipment data may be transferred to partners and authorities in the destination country. Our infrastructure providers may process data outside the Lao PDR. Where required, we rely on appropriate safeguards such as standard contractual clauses.

6. Data retention

  • Account and profile data — kept while your account is active.
  • Shipment and payment records — kept for 2 years after the transaction to meet accounting, tax and customs obligations, even after account closure.
  • Chat and support messages — kept for 1 month.
  • Diagnostic logs — kept for up to 90 days.

After the applicable period we delete the data or irreversibly anonymise it.

7. Security

We use technical and organisational safeguards designed to protect personal data, including:

  • HTTPS/TLS encryption for all traffic between the app and our servers.
  • Encrypted on-device storage for authentication tokens, using the Android Keystore and iOS Keychain.
  • Password hashing and one-time codes for phone verification.
  • Role-based access control, so only authorised staff can view customer records.

No system is perfectly secure. If a breach affects your personal data we will notify you and the competent authority where the law requires it.

8. Your rights

Subject to applicable law, you may access the personal data we hold about you, correct inaccurate data, delete your account and associated data, restrict or object to certain processing, port your data to another provider, withdraw consent for camera, photo or notification access at any time in your device settings, and complain to your local data-protection authority.

Most profile fields can be edited directly in the app under Profile and Edit Profile. To exercise any other right, email zhune.noodle@icloud.com. We respond within 30 days.

9. Account and data deletion

You can request deletion of your CLS Global account and personal data by submitting a request at https://tt-tradingsole.com/account-deletion, or by emailing zhune.noodle@icloud.com from the address or phone number registered to your account.

What is deleted: your account credentials, profile details and photo, chat messages, push notification tokens, and saved addresses and contacts.

What is retained and why: shipment records, invoices and payment references are retained for 2 years in de-identified or restricted form where accounting, tax, customs and transport law requires us to keep them. They are no longer linked to your login and are not used for any other purpose.

Deletion requests are completed within 30 days.

10. Children's privacy

The app is not directed to children under 16 and we do not knowingly collect their personal data. If you believe a child has given us personal data, contact us and we will delete it.

11. Third-party services
